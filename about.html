About Me

Background

Hi, I'm Arindam. I am a systems and computational learner focused on translating theory into real implementations through code, simulations, and interactive systems.

This portfolio focuses on gameplay systems engineering — building modular, server-authoritative systems for multiplayer game environments.

What I Work On

  • Client-Server gameplay architecture
  • Gameplay systems design
  • NPC AI and behavior systems
  • Data persistence and state management
  • Interactive system design

Engineering Approach

My goal is to understand systems deeply — not only how they work, but how they can be modeled, implemented, and extended. I treat programming as a way to explore systems through experimentation, simulation, and real-time environments.

Many of the systems presented here are built with modularity and scalability in mind, allowing them to support complex multiplayer experiences.

Broder Interests

My broader interests include embedded systems, computational physics, mathematical thinking, and game development. Across these areas, I focus on building practical implementations that translate theoretical ideas into working systems.
